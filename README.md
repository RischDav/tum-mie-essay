# TUM Information Engineering Master Essay

During the application procedure for the Information Engineering Master Course (Heilbronn), you have to upload a scientific essay. Here is mine as inspiration for future students. I missed the first round by 4 points and got accepted after the interview. From the list of topics, I have chosen "Assess the Environmental Impact of Large Language Models Beyond Energy Consumption"

> **Important:** I don't know how many points I got for this essay, so don't see this as correct, but rather as inspiration.
